The Republic of Kiribati

The Republic of Kiribati lies at the very center of the Pacific Ocean. Some experts considers Kiribati to be at the very end of the world, because it territories straddles all four hemispheres. So Kiribati is in the Northen hemisphere and Southern hemisphere as well as in the Western and Eastern Hemisphere.The country is made up of 33 tiny coral atolls, they appear as tiny dots on a map, and they strechted over an ocean area roughly the size of continental USA. Distance between the islands is a major problem for the country and has hindered development on various fronts. Life in Kiribati is simple, the people are always happy even though they do not have much in terms of material wealth. The coral reefs around each island provides abundant marine life and this serves as a source of food for most people on these islands. People from Kiribati are not rich, but they are well fed with sea food.
